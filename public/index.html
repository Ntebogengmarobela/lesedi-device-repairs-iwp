A breakdown of Repair Fees

Diagnostic Fee
The fee charged to inspect a device and identify a device and identify its problem. The fee depends on the type of device being repaired
SubTotal
The diagnostic fee plus the cost of the selected repair services before VAT is added.
VAT
Value-Added Tax calculated at 15% of the subtotal.
Total
The subtotal plus 15% VAT, giving the final qouted amount.
